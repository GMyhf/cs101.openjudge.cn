A. Watermelon

Source
Codeforces 4A
Time limit
1 second
Memory limit
64 megabytes
Description

Pete and Billy have a watermelon weighing w kilograms. Can they split it into two positive parts such that both parts have even weights? The two parts do not have to be equal.

Input
The only line contains an integer w (1 ≤ w ≤ 100).
Output
Print YES if such a split is possible; otherwise print NO.
样例输入
8
样例输出
YES
